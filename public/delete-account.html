Request Account & Associated Data Deletion

This page provides official instructions for users of Haze Browser (com.aryandas.haze), developed and maintained by Aryan Das, to request deletion of their optional Google / Firebase Cloud Sync account and all associated data.

01. How to Request Account & Data Deletion

2 Methods Available
  1. Method A.
    Request via Web / Email (Without the App Installed): Submit your request using the form on this page or send an email directly to aryandas.original@gmail.com from the Google Account email address associated with your Haze Browser profile, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - Haze Browser".
  2. Method B.
    Delete Directly Inside Haze Browser (Android App): Open Haze Browser, tap the bottom Menu icon, navigate to Settings → Account & Cloud Sync, and select Delete Account & Cloud Backup. To wipe local device storage, go to Settings → Privacy Shield → Clear All Browsing Data.
  3. Method C.
    Revoke Google OAuth Access Anytime: You can also revoke Haze Browser's Google Sign-In authorization directly at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

02. Data Deleted, Data Retained & Processing Time

0-Day Telemetry
Firebase / Google Account & Cloud Sync Data Permanently Deleted

Includes your Firebase Authentication user record (UID, email address, display name) and all synchronized cloud backup records (bookmarks, folder trees, custom homepage shortcuts, browser preferences, and theme selections).

On-Device Browsing History & Password Vault Deleted Immediately (0-Day)

Local SQLite databases (HazeDatabase), cached web files, cookies, and hardware-encrypted credentials (HazePasswordVault) reside strictly on your device and are erased immediately when cleared in-app or when the app is uninstalled.

Data Retained After Deletion None (0 Bytes Retained)

Haze Browser operates with zero first-party telemetry, zero analytics SDKs, and zero server logs. No personal information, anonymized identifiers, or backup copies are retained after your deletion request is processed.

Expected Processing Time Within 7 Business Days

In-app deletions occur instantaneously. Email and web deletion requests are verified and processed by developer Aryan Das within 7 business days, followed by a confirmation email once deletion is complete.
