01. Core Privacy Principle & Zero-Telemetry Architecture

Haze Browser is engineered from the ground up with a strict zero-telemetry, local-first architecture. Unlike conventional web browsers that continuously transmit browsing history, usage diagnostics, or crash reports to central servers, Haze Browser operates without developer-owned tracking infrastructure.

  • Zero First-Party Telemetry: Haze Browser contains no background telemetry daemons, diagnostic beacons, pingbacks, or usage counters. No server logs of your browsing sessions exist because Haze operates without central user-tracking servers.
  • Zero Third-Party Analytics or Ad SDKs: The application is completely free of Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Adjust, AppsFlyer, Mixpanel, Facebook Graph SDK, or any behavioral profiling SDKs.
  • Zero Hardware / Advertising Identifiers: Haze Browser never queries or collects your Android Advertising ID (AAID), IMEI, hardware serial numbers, or device MAC address.

02. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To function as a full-featured web browser powered by a standalone custom-compiled Chromium Engine (Blink + V8), Haze Browser processes and stores the following user-generated data strictly locally within your device's private application sandbox:

  • Browsing History, Bookmarks, Shortcuts & Omnibox Index (HazeDatabase): Your visited URLs, page titles, custom bookmarks, homepage speed-dial shortcuts, and download queue records are stored locally in an on-device SQLite database (/data/user/0/com.aryandas.haze/databases/). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable optional Cloud Sync.
  • Hardware-Encrypted Password Vault (HazePasswordVault): If you choose to save website login credentials in Haze Browser, they are encrypted on-device using hardware-backed AES-256-GCM keys generated and isolated inside the AndroidKeyStore (Trusted Execution Environment / StrongBox). Viewing or exporting saved credentials requires device biometric or PIN authentication.
  • Cookies, Web Cache & Partitioned Web Storage: First-party cookies and HTTP cache files (RFC 7234) are stored locally so websites can maintain your login state. Third-party tracking cookies are blocked by default via the native C++17 libhazeshield.so engine. All localStorage, sessionStorage, and IndexedDB instances are strictly partitioned by origin.
  • Incognito / Private Browsing Sessions: Incognito tabs operate in an isolated in-memory context. When the last Incognito tab is closed, all associated cookies, DOM storage, and session artifacts are permanently purged from device memory.

03. Optional Google Account Sign-In & Cloud Backup Sync

Haze Browser can be used 100% offline/locally as a guest without creating an account or signing in. However, if you voluntarily choose to sign in using Google Sign-In (via Google Play Services Auth / Firebase Authentication) to synchronize your browser across devices:

  • Account Identifiers Accessed: With your explicit consent, Haze Browser accesses your basic Google Account profile identifier (email address, display name, profile avatar URL, and Firebase Authentication UID) solely to authenticate your sync session.
  • Synchronized Data Scope: Only the specific browser categories you select—such as bookmarks, hierarchical bookmark folders, homepage shortcuts, search preferences, and ambient theme settings—are backed up to your personal Google Cloud / Firebase sync container.
  • No Developer Inspection or Monetization: Your synchronized cloud backup is used exclusively to restore your browser settings across your own devices and is never sold, analyzed, or shared with advertisers.

04. Device Permissions Requested & How They Are Used

Haze Browser adheres to Android zero-trust permission governance. Sensitive runtime permissions are requested only when you actively trigger a feature that requires them:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used only when you launch the built-in QR Code Scanner or explicitly grant a website permission to use your camera (e.g., for WebRTC video calls). Camera video frames are processed locally in real time and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted to the developer.
  • Microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO): Used only when you tap the Voice Search button in the omnibox or explicitly grant a website microphone access for voice/video communication.
  • Notifications & Foreground Media Playback (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): Used by HazeMediaNotificationService (MediaSessionCompat) to provide Lockscreen and Notification Shade playback controls, track metadata, and scrubber bars while audio or video is playing in the browser, as well as to display multi-threaded file download progress.
  • Biometric Authentication (USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT): Used locally via the Android system BiometricPrompt to verify your identity before unlocking or exporting credentials from HazePasswordVault. Haze Browser never accesses raw biometric fingerprint or facial data.
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to load web pages, resolve domains, and detect offline status.

05. Data Sharing & Types of Parties Data Is Shared With

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal or sensitive user data with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers.

Data leaves your device only during direct, user-initiated actions with the following parties:

  • Google LLC (Google Play Services / Firebase Authentication & Cloud Sync): Only if you explicitly opt into Google Account Sign-In and Cloud Backup Sync, your account identifier and synced bookmarks/settings are transmitted over TLS 1.3 to Google's authentication and cloud storage infrastructure.
  • Websites & Search Engines You Visit: When you navigate to a website or submit a search query to your chosen search engine (Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Yahoo, Ecosia, or Wikipedia), your network request is sent directly to that destination web server. Haze Browser automatically attaches DNT: 1 (Do Not Track) and Sec-GPC: 1 (Global Privacy Control) headers and strips known tracking parameters (utm_*, fbclid, gclid) to protect your privacy on third-party sites.

06. Data Security & Cryptographic Safeguards

  • Encryption in Transit: All network connections enforce HTTPS / TLS 1.3 with cleartext HTTP traffic blocked by default in the Android runtime configuration.
  • Encryption at Rest: Saved credentials in HazePasswordVault are encrypted with hardware-isolated AES-256-GCM keys stored in the AndroidKeyStore.
  • Native C++17 Threat Filtering & Sandboxing: Web content runs inside isolated multi-process Chromium WebContents sandboxes protected by libhazeshield.so (compiled with -fstack-protector-strong -D_FORTIFY_SOURCE=2 -fPIE -O3), Canvas/WebGL anti-fingerprinting, and WebRTC LAN IP leak protection.

07. Data Retention & How to Delete Your Account and Data

You retain 100% ownership and control over your data at all times:

  • Instant On-Device Deletion (0-Day Retention): You can permanently erase your browsing history, cached files, cookies, site permissions, and saved passwords at any time inside the app via Settings → Privacy Shield → Clear All Browsing Data, or by uninstalling Haze Browser.
  • In-App Account & Cloud Sync Deletion: You can unlink your Google Account and permanently delete your cloud backup container at any time via Settings → Account & Cloud Sync → Delete Account & Cloud Backup.
  • Web / Email Account Deletion Request (Without the App Installed): If you have already uninstalled Haze Browser and wish to request deletion of your Firebase / Google Cloud Sync account and associated data, visit our dedicated Account & Data Deletion Page (delete-account.html) or email aryandas.original@gmail.com. Requests are processed within 7 business days, after which 0 bytes of user data are retained.

08. Children's Privacy

Haze Browser is a general-audience web browser and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created a Cloud Sync profile without parental consent, please contact us at aryandas.original@gmail.com and we will promptly delete the account and associated data.

09. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA / CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to access, correct, export, or erase your personal data, and to opt out of any sale or behavioral sharing of personal information:

  • Right to Data Portability: Saved credentials in HazePasswordVault can be exported to a standard, unencrypted CSV file at any time on-device after passing biometric authentication.
  • Do Not Sell or Share: Haze Browser never sells or shares personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising and transmits Sec-GPC: 1 automatically.
  • Zero Subprocessors: Aside from optional user-initiated Google Cloud Sync, Haze Browser engages zero third-party data subprocessors.
